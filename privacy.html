Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy outlines how this application handles user information. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using third-party login providers (such as Google Sign-In), we may receive basic profile information shared by the provider, including:

  • Your email address
  • Your public profile name

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to:

  • Create and maintain your user account.
  • Identify you when you log into the application.
  • Provide core services and application functionality.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your information is only processed through our secure database infrastructure (Supabase) to manage your authenticated session.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as your account remains active. You can request the full deletion of your account and all associated profile data at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We implement standard, industry-accepted security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact the developer directly through the support email listed on the application's consent screen.
